(02) — The idea

The idea behind Barterly

Everyone on campus knows something worth sharing. The coder who wants to learn photography. The photographer who needs a website. Barterly — the old idea of barter, rebuilt for skills — connects them, so an hour of what you know buys an hour of what you don’t.

  1. 01

    Knowledge has value

    Your notes, hacks and hobbies can be someone else’s breakthrough.

  2. 02

    Everyone can contribute

    You don’t need to be an expert in everything to teach something useful.

  3. 03

    Growth is reciprocal

    Every exchange moves two people forward, not one.

(05) — Flexible by design

Your learning. Your way.

One-to-one

Focused, personal guidance.

Learn directly from another member at your pace. Perfect for coding doubts, interview prep or getting your first guitar chords right.

Small group

Ideas bounce better together.

Three to six learners, one shared goal. Practise spoken English, crack aptitude sets or jam as a band.

Video call from anywhere, or meet at a spot on campus. You choose what feels comfortable.

(11) — Good questions

Questions, answered.

New to skill exchange? Here’s what people usually ask before their first swap.

What exactly is Barterly?

A community platform where people teach skills they know and learn skills they want — directly with each other, through group sessions, or with time credits.

Do I need to be a professional to teach?

No. Share anything you can confidently help with. Be honest about your level and agree on expectations before you begin.

Do I have to teach the same skill I want to learn?

Not at all. Some exchanges are direct swaps; others use group learning or time credits so different skills can be traded.

Can I learn online and in person?

Yes — video calls, campus meetups, group activities or project-based collaboration, whatever suits both people.

What if I’m just starting out?

Everyone starts somewhere. Join open sessions first, and share a simple skill from your studies or hobbies when you feel ready.

Is every exchange free?

The core idea is reciprocal learning, not payment. Joining Barterly is free; always check the terms of any optional services.
